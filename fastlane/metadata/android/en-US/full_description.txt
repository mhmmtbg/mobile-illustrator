Layered vector drawing on your phone and tablet. Opens Illustrator (.ai), PDF and SVG files with their layers, lets you edit them, and saves back to .ai, PDF, SVG or PNG.

FILES
• Opens .ai (PDF-compatible), .pdf and .svg files; "Open with" from your file manager works too
• Layers arrive with their names and visibility; artboards are kept
• Tabs: keep several documents open at once; copy and paste objects and layers between documents
• Import into document: add the layers of another .ai, PDF or SVG file to the open document
• Saves as .ai, PDF, SVG and transparent PNG
• My documents: all your work in one place with thumbnails; autosave means nothing gets lost

DRAWING AND EDITING
• Pen (Bezier), pencil, rectangle, ellipse, line, text, eyedropper, hand tool
• Node editing: drag anchors and handles, add or delete anchors, switch between corner and smooth
• Move, scale, rotate; numeric size and angle; flip horizontally and vertically
• Align, distribute, and snap with smart guides
• Shape operations: unite, subtract, intersect, exclude; clipping masks
• Group, arrange, duplicate, 200 steps of undo
• Vectorize: turn a logo, drawing or photo into editable paths

COLOUR AND TEXT
• Solid colours, linear and radial gradients, opacity; blend modes in a file are kept
• Stroke width, caps and joins, dashed lines
• Colour wheel and colour codes; CMYK colour picking for print; CMYK and spot colours in a file keep their values
• Group by colour: change the colour of every object that uses it in one place
• Multi-line text, alignment, line and letter spacing; font list with previews, 40 bundled fonts, or load your own .ttf/.otf; convert text to outlines
• Text warp: arc, arch, wave, bulge, rise

TABLET AND PHONE
• On tablets: a side tool rail and a docked layers panel
• Two-finger pan and zoom
• English and Turkish interface

ADS
The app is free and shows ads. "Buy the developer a coffee" is a one-time payment that removes ads for good.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Files must have been saved from Illustrator with "Create PDF Compatible File" (the default)
• Live effects, symbols and brushes arrive flattened; mesh gradients and pattern fills are shown as a flat colour
• CMYK colours on screen are an approximate preview
